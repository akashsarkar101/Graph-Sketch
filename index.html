Edge list ⇄ Graph

How to use

  1. Edge list → graph: type or paste edges (n m then u v lines) in the box.
  2. Draw → edge list: sketch a circle for a node, a line between two nodes for an edge, or tap to drop a node. Scribble over something to rub it out.
  3. Copy the edge list, adjacency list, matrix, Python or C++ output straight into your code.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
